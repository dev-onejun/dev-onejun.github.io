### Building a future-proof foundation with multimode automation ()

* AIOps: The use AI, machine learning (ML), and big data to automate, monitor, and enhance IT infrastructure management

* multimode (e.g. infras for on-prem or public/private cloud..) is required for **automation** as well

#### Multimode in Automation

* **Task-based**
    - 80% of current workloads
    - 'deterministic' automation
    - "who approved this? or audit this?" - security, compliance perspectives.
* **Event-driven**
    - 15% of current workloads and growing
    - w/o human intervention, but still human-in-the-loop
* **Agent-driven**
    - 5% of current workloads and growing
    - explores problems with LLM-powered reasoning, then makes the recommendation
